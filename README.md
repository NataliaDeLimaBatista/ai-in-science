# AI in Science

Readings and practical references on using AI in research, scientific writing, peer review, and science communication.

## Start here

Begin with *Ten Simple Rules for Optimal and Careful Use of Generative AI in Science*. Use the checklist when planning or documenting AI use in a research project.

## What’s inside

- [Research and discovery](research-and-discovery.md) — Research articles, case studies, and perspectives
- [Writing and peer review](writing-and-peer-review.md) — Academic integrity, author voice, peer review, and publication news
- [Science communication](science-communication.md) — Public understanding, accessibility, and trust
- [Publishing policies and reporting](publishing-policies.md) — Guidance from publishers, journals, and research organizations
- [Research checklist](research-checklist.md) — Questions for planning, verification, documentation, and disclosure

## Add a reference

Add the title and a link to the original source in the relevant file. A sentence about what you learned or why it’s worth reading makes a new article or resource easier to revisit.

```markdown
- [Title](https://example.com) — Why it’s worth reading.
```
