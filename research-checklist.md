# Checklist for AI Use in Research

Use this checklist when planning, conducting, or reporting AI-assisted research.

- [ ] Have I checked the policies of the journal, conference, or institution?
- [ ] Have I identified each stage at which I used AI?
- [ ] Have I distinguished writing assistance from AI use as part of the research methods?
- [ ] Have I verified the facts, interpretations, calculations, code, citations, and references?
- [ ] Have I protected the confidentiality of data, documents, peer reviews, and unpublished results?
- [ ] Have I recorded the tool, model or version, purpose, and relevant procedures?
- [ ] Have I kept the files, intermediate results, and records needed for reproducibility?
- [ ] Have I disclosed my use of AI in the required place and format?
- [ ] Have I reviewed the full content, and can I take responsibility for it?

Researchers remain responsible for the work. Verify outputs, protect sensitive information, document procedures, and explain where AI contributed.
